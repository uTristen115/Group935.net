Treyarch Zombies Games

Browse Treyarch Zombies games, maps, Easter eggs, relics, wonder weapons, perks, songs, characters, and story files.

World at War (2008)

Call of Duty: World at War. Nacht der Untoten, Verrückt, Shi No Numa, and Der Riese.

  • Nacht der Untoten - Unknown bunker, 1945
  • Verrückt - Wittenau Sanatorium, Berlin, 1945
  • Shi No Numa - Rising Sun Facility, Imperial Japan
  • Der Riese - Group 935 main facility, Breslau

Black Ops (2010)

Black Ops expanded Zombies into a full mode with seven maps spanning the globe. The Aether storyline took shape as players traveled from Kino to the Pentagon, through the cosmodrome and Shangri-La, and ultimately to Moon where Richtofen enacted his grand scheme.

  • Kino der Toten - Abandoned cinema, East Germany, 1960s
  • Five - The Pentagon, Washington D.C., 1963
  • Ascension - Soviet cosmodrome, 1963
  • Call of the Dead - Siberian coast, 2011
  • Shangri-La - Himalayan jungle temple
  • Moon - Griffin Station, lunar far side

Black Ops II (2012)

Black Ops II introduced the Victis crew navigating a post-apocalyptic Earth after the events of the Moon Easter Egg, and featured some of the most iconic maps of all time in Mob of the Dead, and Origins.

  • TranZit - Hanford, Washington, post-Moon Earth
  • Die Rise - Shanghai, post-Moon Earth
  • Mob of the Dead - Alcatraz Island, 1933
  • Buried - Angolan mine, post-Moon Earth
  • Origins - Excavation Site 64, Northern France

Black Ops III (2015)

Black Ops III followed Primis across the multiverse to collect the souls of their Ultimis counterparts. Featured Gobblegums, Widow's Wine, four elemental bows in Der Eisendrache, and the conclusion of the cycle in Revelations.

  • Shadows of Evil - Morg City, 1940s
  • The Giant - Group 935 facility, 1945
  • Der Eisendrache - Austrian mountain castle, 1945
  • Zetsubou No Shima - Pohnpei Island, Pacific theater
  • Gorod Krovi - Stalingrad, 1945
  • Revelations - The Aether

Black Ops 4 (2018)

Black Ops 4 ran two storylines in parallel: the Aether story concluded with Blood of the Dead and Tag der Toten, while the Chaos story introduced a new crew chasing artifacts through IX, Voyage of Despair, Dead of the Night, and Ancient Evil.

  • IX - Roman coliseum, Chaos timeline
  • Voyage of Despair - RMS Titanic, 1912
  • Blood of the Dead - Alcatraz pocket dimension
  • Dead of the Night - Rhodes estate, England
  • Ancient Evil - Delphi, Greece
  • Tag der Toten - Siberian facility

Cold War (2020)

Cold War began anew with a new Dark Aether storyline. Requiem and Omega Group investigated dimensional breaches across four round-based maps and Outbreak. Introduced weapon rarity, armor, field upgrades, and an open progression system shared with multiplayer.

  • Die Maschine - Projekt Endstation, Morasko, 1984
  • Firebase Z - Outpost 25, Vietnam, 1984
  • Mauer der Toten - East Berlin, 1984
  • Forsaken - Ural Mountains, 1984

Black Ops 6 (2024)

Black Ops 6 continued the Dark Aether storyline with Maya Aguinaldo and the Requiem team investigating the Order across six maps from Terminus to The Reckoning. It introduced Augments for perks, new Ammo Mods, and the return of round-based Zombies mechanics after MWZ.

  • Terminus - Atlantic prison facility, 1991
  • Liberty Falls - West Virginia, 1991
  • Citadelle des Morts - Alsace, 1991
  • The Tomb - Eastern Europe, 1991
  • Shattered Veil - Liberty Falls perimeter, 1991
  • The Reckoning - Janus facility, 1991

Black Ops 7 (2025)

Black Ops 7 sucks the characters into the depths of the Dark Aether. As they arrive, Requiem encounters a new version of the original crew. Together they have freed the Shadowsmiths at Ashes of the Damned, Astra Malorum, and Totenreich.

  • Ashes of the Damned - Dark Aether construct
  • Astra Malorum - Mountaintop research site
  • Paradox Junction - Liminal transit hub
  • Totenreich - Aetheric reconstruction
