# Totenreich Easter Egg

Game: Black Ops 7 Zombies  
Map: Totenreich  
Status: Rewritten from verified steps  
Main reward path: Complete the Totenreich main quest

## Jotun Star Setup

1. Restore power and unlock Pack-a-Punch.

2. Head to Dry Dock and use the green crane control inside the crane building.

3. The crane lowers a wall near the ship. Use the lowered wall to reach the ship tip, then hold the prompt there to recover the chain.

4. Search the crate behind the truck on the bridge for the Chili Chunks.

5. Set the Chili Chunks on the Market Center table.

6. After placing the Chili Chunks, play until the second special round. Partway into that round, kill the Zursa that appears and take its lantern.

7. Bring the lantern to Burial Grounds. Use the chain on the left side of the door to open it, revealing the altar inside.

8. Place the lantern on the altar.

9. Compare the altar constellations with the wall symbols, then select the matching constellations in this positional order:

   1. Left
   2. Right
   3. Back
   4. Front

   If the dog symbol appears in the left position, for example, choose dog first.

10. Astrid will begin moving through the area. Stay with her and clear the frozen zombie stops.

11. Complete three Astrid stops.

12. After the escort ends, climb the Lighthouse route and claim the Jotun Star at the top.

## Main Quest

### 1. Open The Lab

1. Travel to the Core Foundry. Up some metal stairs along the wall, find the covered wall vent and shoot the cover to pop it off.

2. Drive an RC-XD through the duct, down the stairs, and over the ramp. No boosting is required to get across. Travel under the garage door on the other side, then detonate the RC-XD next to the explosive barrels in the room to open it. The entry is to the left of Pack-a-Punch.

   TIP: A free RC-XD spawns in Eidskallen Square.

### 2. Recover The Lab Sample

1. From the lab near Pack-a-Punch, look at the numbered doors just outside the playable space. Two of them will have radioactive symbols beside them. Note those numbers.

2. Convert the two numbers into letters using the alphabet: 1=A, 2=B, 3=C, 4=D, and so on.

3. Identify your letters, then search the room for jars marked with those letters.

4. Place the two jars in the device next to the closed jail cell to create the solution.

5. Use the solution to melt the body on the bench across the room, revealing the cell key.

6. Open the cell and retrieve the uranium sample.

### 3. Secure The Fishing Canister

1. Find a fishing rod. Check Dry Dock, Storm Bridge, Fishery Island, and Beacon Island.

2. Check the fishing spots for a glowing green fish jumping at the surface.

3. Fish at that spot until the green fish drops. A Ravager will appear and take it, shoot the Ravager as it leaves.

4. On the next round, a Radiated Ravager will spawn in a few locations around the map.

5. Find the Radiated Ravager in each location it moves to and damage it whenever you catch up to it.

6. When the Radiated Ravager dies, collect the uranium canister it drops.

### 4. Pull The Final Canister From A Glocke Drop

1. Call a Glocke Drop onto a full zombie group.

2. Kill the zombies suspended in the field.

3. Collect the third uranium canister.

### 5. Arm The Lighthouse Cannon

1. Take the crowbar from the second floor of the Lighthouse.

2. In the War Factory Admin Room, find the shipping manifest on a shelf. Look for the ammo icon on the manifest and remember the number associated with it.

3. Search the numbered crates around the map, then use the crowbar to open the crate matching the manifest number.

   Known crate locations:

   1. Core Foundry
   2. Dry Dock
   3. War Factory
   4. Fjord Road

4. Collect the artillery shell from the matching crate.

5. Load the recovered shell into the artillery cannon next to the Lighthouse, facing the Robot head at spawn.

6. Hit the cannon with the Jotun Star to fire it.

7. Travel to the Robot head and collect the Signal Amplifier.

### 6. Calibrate The Rockets And Open The Dry Dock Building

1. Install the Signal Amplifier in Tyr's head.

2. Two lights below Tyr's catwalk will start flashing.

3. There will be two number sequences. Make note of each one.

   Example: if the left light blinks twice and the right light blinks three times, the first code is 2-3.

4. Use the noted sequences to tune the Radio Tower dials. Put the left light number on the left dial and the right light number on the right dial.

5. Rockets will fire. If they hit the Radio Tower, the calibration is correct. If they miss, enter the second sequence.

6. Once the rockets are calibrated, collect a Rocket Barrage Controller.

7. Use the Rocket Barrage Controller on the large building with blocked stairs marked 02 in Dry Dock to open it.

### 7. Charge The Atomkraft Core

1. Enter the newly opened location at Dry Dock.

2. Use the claw machine in the opened room to pick up the uranium cores and slot them into the grid on the right.

3. Arrange the cores so the meter lands in the green zone, then collect the Atomkraft Core.

4. Carry the Atomkraft Core to Quick Revive at spawn and place it on the box just to the right.

5. Enter the building behind Quick Revive and interact with the generator to turn it on.

6. Defend the core while it charges.

7. During the charge, the generator will stop. Return to it and interact again to resume charging.

8. Once finished, take the charged Atomkraft Core to the bridge. You cannot travel by zipline while carrying it.

### 8. Cook The Dravakar Shard

1. Place the Atomkraft Core on top of the barrel with a yellow stripe facing Tyr.

2. Tyr will retrieve the canister and fire his laser at Dravakar.

3. A Dravakar Shard will drop to the left of the bridge, near Tyr's foot.

4. Collect the shard and take it to the church where Vulture Aid is.

5. Place the shard on the spit over the fire.

6. Fire a charged shot from the Jotun Star to start the fire.

7. Once the fire is lit, use a Disciple Injection to pick up zombies and throw them at the fire.

8. Continue throwing zombies into the fire until a lockdown event starts.

9. Fight the HVT Necropincer and take the Sunstone it drops.

### 9. Activate The Symbol Dial

1. Take the Sunstone to the church near Eidskallen Square.

2. Place it on the altar in the center and melee it with the Jotun Star to activate the Symbol Dial above the church.

3. Review the direction of the arrows in the sky. There are three arrows, each with lines near the center of the dial.

4. Read the arrows by their line count: the arrow with one line is arrow 1, the arrow with two lines is arrow 2, and the arrow with three lines is arrow 3.

5. Around the map, find the symbols from the dial floating in the air.

6. Hit the symbols with charged shots from the Jotun Star in the order the arrows direct you.

7. If done incorrectly, the arrows will provide new symbols and you can try again.

### 10. Enter The Boss Fight

1. After the correct symbol sequence is entered, enter Tyr's head.

2. Make sure you are adequately prepared with perks, Pack-a-Punch III, legendary rarity weapons, and any GobbleGums you want for the fight.

3. Interact with Tyr's computer to start the boss fight.
