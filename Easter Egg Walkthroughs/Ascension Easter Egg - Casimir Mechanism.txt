


Casimir Mechanism

Requires: 4 players, a Pack-a-Punched Ray Gun or Ray Gun Mark II in BO3, the Thundergun, Matryoshka Dolls, and Gersh Devices.


Step 1: Activate the Power and Launch the Rocket

Turn on the power and link all three Lunar Landers. 
Lunar Lander Ф (F) is located in the Scrapyard near Stamin-up 
Lunar Lander Б (B) near Speed Cola and the Sickle 
Lunar Lander Д (D) near PhD Flopper in BO or Widow’s Wine in BO3. 

Once all three landers are linked, return to the power switch and launch the rocket.

Next, obtain Gersh Devices from the Mystery Box, and throw one at the generator near PhD Flopper in BO or Widow’s Wine in BO3. The generator is located just outside the map boundary.



Step 2: Activate the First Node

Travel over to stamin-up and enter the scrapyard near Lunar Lander Ф (F). Under the metal stairs you will find a computer monitor showing static. 

Interact to reveal the pyramid of the Illuminati. This completes Node 1, and the first light on the Casimir Mechanism will turn on. 



Step 3: Press the Four Buttons

This step must be completed on a Space Monkey round. 

Once the round begins, four buttons will appear around the map. They are located:

On the wall near Stamin-up
On the wall opposite Juggernog
On the wall in the room with PhD Flopper (BO) or Widow's Wine (BO3)
On the wall near Speed Cola. 

Each player must find one button. Once everyone is ready, all four buttons must be pressed at the same time.

If done correctly, the buttons will sink into the walls. This completes Node 2, and the second light on the Casimir Mechanism will turn on.



Step 4: Stand on the Pressure Plate

Go to the area just outside the Pack-a-Punch room. You will find a circular platform that acts as a pressure plate, with a clock on the wall nearby.

All four players must stand on this plate for 2 minutes.

Once completed, a Nuke power-up will activate. This completes Node 3, and the third light on the Casimir Mechanism will turn on.



Step 5: Spell LUNA
Next, the players must spell LUNA by collecting floating letters while riding the Lunar Landers.

Start with the lander parked at Spawn and choose one player to ride it. The rider should stay on the lander while the other players call it between locations.

Call the lander in this order:

First, call the lander from Spawn to Lunar Lander Ф (F) near Stamin-Up to collect the letter L.
Next, call the lander back to Spawn to collect the letter U.
Then, call the lander to Lunar Lander Б (B) near Speed Cola to collect the letter N.
Finally, call the lander back to Lunar Lander Ф (F) near Stamin-Up to collect the letter A.

Once LUNA has been collected, Node 4 is complete, and the fourth light on the Casimir Mechanism will turn on.



Step 6: Free Gersh

Once all four nodes are complete, a fifth light will appear on the ground in front of the Casimir Mechanism. This is where the required weapons are used.

It is best to plan this step ahead of time so every player knows their role.

First, throw a Gersh Device as close to the white light on the ground as possible.

Once the Gersh Device is active, all four players must fire their assigned weapons at it to power it up.

Required weapons:

1 upgraded Thundergun, the Zeus Cannon
2 upgraded Ray Guns, Porter’s X2 Ray Gun
1 set of Matryoshka Dolls

If this process is done correctly, Gersh’s soul will be freed, and the Easter Egg has been completed.

In the original Black Ops version, all players are rewarded with a Death Machine for 90 seconds. In the Black Ops III Zombies Chronicles version, all players are rewarded with every perk.
