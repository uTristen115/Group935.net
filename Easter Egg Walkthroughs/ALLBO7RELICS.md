Music Box (TR) * Hardest Wicked Relic By Requirements Along With Dragon *

Relic Effect: Enemies can only be eliminated with critical kills.

Unlock Route:

Get to round 60, enter Tyr’s head and get 5 headshots total within 1 minute.

Trial Portal Location: Dry Dock, very west side by secret blocked staircase and near Fjord Road.

Trial: Locate Holdout Locations and Survive.

Waves 1, 2, 3, 4: Kill enemies to move up progress bar. About 2-3 Necropincers will spawn on each wave.

Waves 5: About 6-8 Necropincers will spawn.

Wave 6: About 3-4 Necropincers will spawn, but holdout circle very small.

Holdout circle will shrink every wave.

Difficulty: Hard (5 headshot sniper requirement and holdout circles are small)

Save and Quit Possible? Yes! Save and quit is possible anytime before round 60!

Augments For Relic: Any

Helpful Tips for Relic:

Save two zombies on the Round 60 frost round and get the following items …

Equipment: 3x Decoys

Gobblegums: Idle Eyes, Kill Joy, Temporal Gift

Kill the two zombies in Spawn, Eidskallen Square or Fishery Island. The point is to start Round 61+ in an area where you are close to the desired location and you can spawn in enough zombies.

Make your way to Skallen Market. Once your zombies have respawned, move in front of the north door of the church. Follow the exact process shown FOR SOLO, this works virtually 100% of the time …

Throw a decoy on the snow in front of the door. Pop kill joy, then throw another decoy in the same spot.

Then, pop Idle Eyes and enter Tyr’s Head immediately. You should have 20-30 zombies ready to shoot. Now its on your sniping ability to get the 5 headshots.

This relic trigger is finicky and is the second hardest in the game behind the spider relic for solo.
