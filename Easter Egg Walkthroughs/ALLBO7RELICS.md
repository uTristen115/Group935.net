## Grim Relics

### Lawyer's Pen

- Map: Ashes of the Damned
- Difficulty: Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode required; no Cursed Tier required.
- Effect: Loot-looking Mimic props can appear around the map. Approaching one can wake it up and turn a simple pickup check into a Mimic fight.
- Portal: Barnhouse south side, beside the left door.
- Save note: Unsafe after candle progress begins.
- Trial: Four Shock Mimic waves. Waves 1 and 3 are progress waves, while waves 2 and 4 ask you to burn down Mimic HVTs. Doppelghasts can join the HVT zombie waves, so do not tunnel only on the Mimic.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 20.
1. Light the Lost Cabins candle inside the ruined fog cabin.
1. Light the Farm House candle at Vandorn Farm.
1. Light the Ashwood candle on the shelf next to Juggernog.
1. Enter the Barnhouse portal after all three candles are lit.

Field notes:
- Molotovs and Napalm Burst are the safest candle tools because they light candles without forcing a special weapon setup.
- The candles can be lit in any order. Keep one or two zombies alive so you can move cleanly between regions without the round flipping.
- Avoid throwing equipment directly into Mimic shock bursts because the burst can erase equipment before it does useful work.
- Brain Rot is the cleanest ammo mod here because Mimics are weak to it and turned zombies help control the cramped waves.
- A medium-range Pack-a-Punched bullet weapon is enough; save explosives or scorestreaks for emergency HVT zombie pressure.

### Dragon Wings

- Map: Ashes of the Damned
- Difficulty: Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode required; no Cursed Tier required.
- Effect: Standard power-up drops stop appearing, including special-round ammo drops, so ammo planning matters more on longer runs.
- Portal: Northwest side of the Farmhouse.
- Save note: Unsafe after the airborne target sequence.
- Trial: Power-ups become hazards and damage you on contact. Waves 1 and 3 build progress, while waves 2 and 4 bring Mimic HVTs.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 20, then stage at Vandorn Farm with a fast-handling bullet weapon.
1. Use the jump pad that launches from Vandorn Farm toward Janus Towers.
1. During the launch, shoot the three purple airborne targets before landing.
1. Enter the Farmhouse portal after the airborne target sequence succeeds.

Field notes:
- Treat the target sequence like one continuous jump rather than three separate attempts.
- Raise look sensitivity before the jump if the targets feel too tight.
- Bring a light, fast-handling bullet weapon for the target sequence rather than a launcher or slow Wonder Weapon.
- Inside the trial, path around power-ups like floor traps. Do not slide through drops unless you are certain they are outside your movement lane.
- Mimic HVT waves are still the real danger; keep armor topped off before entering so a bad power-up path does not stack with HVT zombie damage.

### Teddy Bear

- Map: Ashes of the Damned
- Difficulty: Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode required; no Cursed Tier required.
- Effect: The pause between rounds is heavily shortened, giving less time to reload, plate, buy ammo, or rotate before the next spawn set begins.
- Portal: Vandorn Farm, on the south wall of the Mystery Box building.
- Save note: Unsafe once the bear hunt starts.
- Trial: Every weapon shot spends essence. Waves 1 and 3 build progress, and waves 2 and 4 bring Mimic HVTs that can drain your points fast if you spray.

Before you start:
- Equip Aether Shroud and bring the Necrofluid Gauntlet.
- You need 10 Aether Shroud charges, one for each Mr. Peeks doll. Power Keg is recommended to speed up the recharge work.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 20 before starting the Mr. Peeks doll collection.
1. Collect the 10 Mr. Peeks dolls in any order. For each doll, charge Aether Shroud, activate it, then grab the doll with the Necrofluid Gauntlet while shrouded.
1. Check these doll locations: Janus Tower Plaza gate toward Blackwater Lake, Vandorn Farm silo, Blackwater Lake upstairs bathroom near Speed Cola, Lost Cabins rooftop on the Ashwood side, Ashwood Church wall opening, Rabbit Alley burning room, Zarya Cosmodrome electric pole, Zarya Cosmodrome room beside the Crafting Table, Grounded Ship barrel area, and the Exit 115 highway sign.
1. Enter the Vandorn Farm portal after the final doll is collected.

Field notes:
- Start and finish the doll hunt in one session. A save during the hunt can break collection progress.
- The Necrofluid Gauntlet keeps the arena cheap because it reduces how often you need to pay for gunfire.
- Spend, bank, or stage your essence before entering. A long panic spray can erase a point stack quickly.
- Use traps or high-value shots where possible, and save full-auto fire for Mimics that must die immediately.

### Gong

- Map: Astra Malorum
- Difficulty: Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode required; no Cursed Tier required.
- Effect: Field Upgrades stop charging passively. You begin with a charge, but Full Power and other forced recharge tools become the main way back.
- Portal: Outside Pack-a-Punch, left of the Gobblegum machine.
- Save note: Safe, but rod-zombie progress must be repeated after loading back in.
- Trial: Only electric damage counts. Waves 1 and 3 build progress; wave 2 can pressure you with multiple Klaus zombies, and wave 4 can bring an Uber Klaus zombie.

Before you start:
- Equip Tesla Storm before starting the bulb sequence.
- Put Dead Wire on a weapon before starting the bulb sequence.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 20 with Tesla Storm equipped and Dead Wire on a weapon.
1. Start a fresh round and identify the rod-marked zombie.
1. Check the bulb above the Pack-a-Punch doorway toward the portal, the Luminarium doorway near Juggernog, and the Machina Astralis or library doorway near the Mars path.
1. Move the rod-marked zombie in front of a target bulb, trigger Tesla Storm and Dead Wire weapon damage, then kill it close to the bulb.
1. Repeat the rod-marked zombie kill for all three bulbs.
1. Enter the Pack-a-Punch portal after all three bulbs are charged.

Field notes:
- A rod-marked zombie needs to die close enough to a charged bulb for that bulb to register.
- If the rod-marked zombie dies too far away, the bulb can fail to charge and the zombie may not return that round.
- Dead Wire with Light Strike and Haste makes the bulb setup more forgiving because the electric trigger can happen on demand more often.
- Tesla Storm duration or radius augments can cover a mistake if the horde piles up while you are positioning a rod-marked zombie.
- Ping the rod-marked zombie for co-op tracking, and avoid splash damage until it is standing where you want it.

### Seed

- Map: Astra Malorum
- Difficulty: Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode required; no Cursed Tier required.
- Effect: The Mystery Box is disabled.
- Portal: Observatory Dome, left side near the Ammo Cache.
- Save note: Unsafe; the pistol can disappear after a reload.
- Trial: Fight through four waves with a Pack-a-Punch I pistol. Waves 1 and 3 are progress waves; the HVT zombie waves can include Klaus zombies, a Mimic, and finally an Uber Klaus zombie.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 20, then pick up the grey pistol only when you are ready to count kills.
1. Kill exactly the same number of zombies as the round number when you collected the pistol. For example, a pickup on round 20 means exactly 20 pistol kills.
1. Check the Observatory Dome wall or lower telescope area, the Luminarium dirt pile or Ammo Cache path, the Machina Astralis bar counter, and the Archives of Orbis bookshelf lane for the pistol.
1. Finish the round with a different weapon once the pistol kill count matches the pickup round.
1. Enter the Observatory Dome portal after the round ends.

Field notes:
- Use the weapon stats screen to verify the pistol count, and stop immediately when it matches the round.
- Avoid passive kill sources like Elemental Pop or Wisp Tea while counting because they can muddy the exact pistol total.
- Rarity upgrades and Insta-Kill help the pistol feel less weak, but Pack-a-Punching during the count can be risky if your setup has shown pickup glitches.
- Bring a second weapon specifically for finishing the round after the pistol count is locked.

### Rocket Relic

- Map: Paradox Junction
- Difficulty: Medium
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode required; no Cursed Tier required.
- Effect: Scorestreaks cannot be used.
- Portal: Past Nuketown, upstairs in Green House while facing south.
- Save note: Unsafe; pet progress does not persist cleanly.
- Trial: Zombies only take meaningful damage from their elemental weaknesses. Waves 1 and 3 build progress; HVT zombie waves usually mix Rad Hound zombies, Doppelghasts, and Mimics.

Unlock steps:
1. Before round 20, use past Nuketown to turn a Rad Hound zombie friendly and pet it.
1. On round 20, move to destroyed or future Nuketown and deploy a D.A.W.G. scorestreak.
1. Put the D.A.W.G. in a stable position, save a zombie, and pet it as the round state resolves so the interaction registers.
1. Enter the Green House portal after both pet interactions register.

Field notes:
- Psych Grenades are the cleanest way to make the friendly Rad Hound zombie pet window predictable.
- Look directly at the pet target during the interact prompt. Side angles can miss the prompt and waste the round setup.
- Do the Rad Hound zombie and D.A.W.G. steps in one run; pet progress is not a save-safe chain.
- Carry separate ammo mods instead of relying on one weapon: Shadow Rift for toxic zombies, Light Mend for Doppelghasts, and Brain Rot for Mimics.
- Golden Armor, a self-revive, and Aether Shroud give the trial room for mistakes when the wrong weakness is in front of you.
- Do not spend the whole trial swapping blindly. Identify the HVT zombie, then commit the weapon that matches its weakness.

### Power Switch

- Map: Totenreich
- Difficulty: Very Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode required; no Cursed Tier required.
- Effect: Your lethal and tactical equipment changes at the start of each round, so long-term equipment planning becomes unreliable.
- Portal: Blodheim Hall, south-wall bear pelt.
- Save note: Safe.
- Trial: Trap kills only. The trial is only three waves, so lead zombies through the map machinery instead of trying to finish them with weapons.

Before you start:
- Bring Scavenger Aid and a Combat Axe, the tomahawk-style lethal, before starting the shield sequence.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 20, then check the deer-head altars outside the playable map at Eidskallen Landing, Dry Dock, Tyr's Foot, and Burial Grounds.
1. Count the skulls on each deer-head altar to determine the shield order.
1. Go to the Stave Church and use Scavenger Aid to read the shields.
1. Throw the Combat Axe at the shields in the order given by the deer-head altar skull counts.
1. Enter the Blodheim Hall portal after the shield sequence is accepted.

Field notes:
- A scoped weapon helps read distant altar skull counts.
- If an outside-map altar is hard to read, rotate to a safer sightline and zoom in rather than guessing under pressure.
- Write down the skull counts before entering the Stave Church, because the shield order is easy to lose under pressure.
- Bank at least 10,000 essence so repeated Flammenfalle trap activations do not strand the run.
- Train zombies tightly before starting a trap cycle so the activation produces enough progress.
- This is one of the safest relic arenas as long as you resist the reflex to shoot weakened zombies.

## Sinister Relics

### Vril Sphere

- Map: Ashes of the Damned
- Difficulty: Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 1, 2, or 3 required.
- Effect: The perk limit drops to four unless another reward bypasses the cap.
- Portal: Zarya Cosmodrome, high middle road while looking east.
- Save note: Safe before round 40.
- Trial: Purchases are disabled during the arena. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; waves 2 and 5 bring Mimic and Doppelghast HVT waves.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 40, then isolate a Doppelghast without killing it outright.
1. Lower its health before starting the jump-pad setup.
1. Lead the damaged Doppelghast onto any jump pad and launch with it.
1. Let the jump-pad landing finish the Doppelghast.
1. Enter the Zarya Cosmodrome portal after the landing kill registers.

Field notes:
- The jump-pad landing needs to finish the Doppelghast, so do not leave it at full health.
- Stay close enough that the Doppelghast follows the jump path. If it falls behind or dies early, repeat with another Doppelghast.
- Open doors, buy armor, fill ammo, and craft equipment before entering because the trial shuts purchases off.
- A Pack-a-Punch III Necrofluid Gauntlet can carry most of the trial while keeping ammo spending predictable.
- Use the lower Cosmodrome training space before entering to reset armor and equipment if the jump-pad setup took damage.

### Samantha's Drawing

- Map: Ashes of the Damned
- Difficulty: Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 1, 2, or 3 required.
- Effect: Your weapon is exchanged each round for a comparable rarity and Pack-a-Punch level.
- Portal: Zarya Cosmodrome, along the path toward Yuri's Lab.
- Save note: Unsafe once Chompy progress has started.
- Trial: Ammo Caches and Max Ammo drops are unavailable. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; waves 2 and 5 can lean heavily on Doppelghast HVTs, with Mimics possible.

Unlock steps:
1. Before round 40, feed Chompy one weapon from each rarity tier: common, uncommon, rare, epic, and legendary.
1. On round 40 or later, place a Wonder Weapon into the Chompy bin to finally open the portal.
1. Enter the Zarya Cosmodrome portal after the Wonder Weapon deposit registers.

Field notes:
- Start with a grey pistol as early as possible so the common tier is not left until the round climb.
- Listen for Chompy before depositing a weapon. If the bin is quiet, do not sacrifice an important gun into the wrong state.
- Do the chain in one session. The rarity sacrifices are not reliable after saving.
- Mule Kick with extra stock ammo makes the no-ammo-cache trial more forgiving.
- Enter with full magazines and a weapon that has comfortable reserves, because a long HVT zombie wave can punish reload-heavy builds.
- Keep Wonder Weapon ammo for HVT zombies instead of spending it on low-threat zombies.

### Focusing Stone

- Map: Ashes of the Damned
- Difficulty: Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 1, 2, or 3 required.
- Effect: Self-revive kits are disabled.
- Portal: Zarya Cosmodrome, beneath the rocket while facing west.
- Save note: Safe before round 40.
- Trial: Your essence is removed when the trial begins. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; waves 2 and 5 can bring Doppelghast and Mimic HVTs.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 40, then damage a Zursa until a knife strike will finish it.
1. Collect the bottle dropped by the knife-finished Zursa.
1. On round 40 or later, complete the legendary TEDD Task and collect the second bottle near the task reward.
1. Carry both bottles to the Blackwater Lake wine bar.
1. After placing the bottles, watch the burst order immediately. When the bottles reset, shoot them in the same order to open the portal.

Field notes:
- The TEDD Task bottle can appear near the task reward rather than directly on top of it.
- Pay attention the instant the bottles are placed, because the order cue happens quickly.
- Spend or craft before entering the trial; any unspent essence will be stripped anyway.
- A Pack-a-Punch III Necrofluid Gauntlet keeps the no-essence trial simple because you are less dependent on ammo buys.

### Spider Fang

- Map: Astra Malorum
- Difficulty: Hard
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 1, 2, or 3 required.
- Effect: Perk prices stop falling over time.
- Portal: Archives of Orbis, opposite Stamin-Up.
- Save note: Unsafe once OSCAR health setup is underway.
- Trial: All perks are stripped when the trial starts. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; wave 2 can bring an Uber Klaus zombie, and wave 5 can bring OSCAR.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 40 or later without advancing main quest steps that can disrupt OSCAR.
1. Activate Wisp Tea so a wisp is active on OSCAR.
1. Lower OSCAR carefully, then make sure a wisp is active on OSCAR before the final hit.
1. Kill OSCAR with a melee weapon while Wisp Tea is active.
1. Enter the Archives of Orbis portal after the OSCAR melee finisher registers.

Field notes:
- Progression state can make the OSCAR setup much less predictable.
- Use Wisp Tea augments that keep the wisp from wasting normal zombies and extend the active window.
- If OSCAR survives into very high rounds, use controlled Ray Gun or knife damage to lower health, then stop early enough that the melee finisher is still possible.
- A legendary Pack-a-Punch III knife gives the cleanest final-hit attempt.
- Insta-Kill GobbleGums and Aether Shroud make the melee finisher much easier to secure.
- The Apogee Annihilator and War Machines help lower HVT zombie health during the trial after your perks are removed.
- Be patient with this setup. It is one of the most sensitive relic unlocks, and rushing the last hit usually causes the miss.

### Matryoshka Doll

- Map: Astra Malorum
- Difficulty: Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 1, 2, or 3 required.
- Effect: Salvage drops are cut down.
- Portal: Machina Astralis, by the Cryopod.
- Save note: Safe after Mars is open and before round 40.
- Trial: Zombie kills only count while a Field Upgrade is active. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; later HVT zombie waves can include Uber Klaus and OSCAR.

Unlock steps:
1. Open the Mars portal before round 40.
1. On Mars, place C4 on the three meat piles around the lower center stage.
1. Train a large zombie horde onto the stage before detonating.
1. Detonate the C4 when the horde is centered and the stage is clear enough for you to survive the blast window.
1. After the detonation registers, leave Mars and check the Machina Astralis portal area.

Field notes:
- Opening the Mars portal before round 40 keeps the high-round setup shorter and safer.
- The trigger appears to care about a meaningful zombie group kill rather than a single cleanup explosion.
- Aether Shroud is the easiest progress button because the active window lets you kill zombies without taking much return fire.
- Frenzied Guard is the stronger co-op or safety option if you need armor and zombie control.
- Do not waste the Field Upgrade on low-density spawns. Gather a wave, activate, then spend the full window.

### Summoning Key

- Map: Paradox Junction
- Difficulty: Medium
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 1, 2, or 3 required.
- Effect: Zombies explode when killed, and the blast can damage you if you stay too close to the body.
- Portal: Yellow House, southwest outside wall.
- Save note: Unsafe after collecting the dog collar.
- Trial: Each wave has a timer, and every zombie must die before time expires. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress, while waves 2 and 5 can mix Rad Hound zombies with Doppelghast HVTs.

Unlock steps:
1. In Pre-Nuke Nuketown, throw a grenade into the broken chimney near the clock and pick up the dog collar that drops.
1. In the Yellow House backyard on Pre-Nuke Nuketown, use Brain Rot to turn a Rad Hound zombie.
1. Let the turned Rad Hound zombie dig up the ball, then collect the ball.
1. On round 40 or later, down yourself in the garden behind the Green House so the grave rises.
1. Interact with the grave to place the dog collar and ball.
1. Enter the Yellow House portal after the grave interaction opens it.

Field notes:
- Equivalent Exchange or Aftertaste can protect the intentional down and keep the setup from costing the run.
- A low-rarity Brain Rot pistol is less likely to kill the Rad Hound zombie before it digs up the ball.
- Ray Gun, Kazimirs, Free Throw, and War Machine pressure are excellent for timed clears.
- Keep distance after zombie kills during both setup and trial, because the relic effect turns close-range cleanup into chip damage.

### Stuffed Elephant

- Map: Totenreich
- Difficulty: Medium
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 1, 2, or 3 required.
- Effect: Health regeneration takes longer to begin.
- Portal: Fishery Island, southeast corner behind the eastern building.
- Save note: Unsafe; complete the no-perk run in one session.
- Trial: Find cursed eggs, destroy them, and kill the HVT zombie before the timer ends. The target sequence can rotate through Necropincers, Doppelghasts, Amalgam zombies, and Zursa.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 40 without buying perks from machines or taking perk rewards that could invalidate the no-perk state.
1. Once round 40 is reached cleanly, rotate to Fishery Island and check for the portal before buying anything.

Field notes:
- Perkaholic before the first round marker may sometimes slip through, but do not rely on that for a clean tutorial attempt.
- Cursed Tier III and Pack-a-Punch IV planning make the no-perk climb less fragile.
- Toxic Growth can hold safer areas like the jail cell or upper Lighthouse while you build points.
- Look for eggs at Beacon Island or Lighthouse, Skallen Market, Storm Bridge near Tyr's Foot, and the Dry Dock Mystery Box room.
- Toxic Growth and a high-tier Jotunn Star make the no-perk climb safer.
- Do not take perks from machines, GobbleGums, TEDD Tasks, or Mr. Peeks if you want the reliable portal spawn.
- Learn the egg sightlines before starting the timed trial, because the timer punishes searching from scratch.

### Dancing Arnie

- Map: Totenreich
- Difficulty: Easy
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 1, 2, or 3 required.
- Effect: Perk machines become cursed and dispense random perks.
- Portal: Fishery Island, northeast corner opposite the Mystery Box spot.
- Save note: Unsafe once the recipe chain begins.
- Trial: Melee kills only. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; HVT zombie waves can bring Necropincers first, then Necropincer and Zursa later.

Unlock steps:
1. Bring the recipe items to the Blodheim Hall firepit in order.
1. Collect the pot under the southwest Skallen Market food stand and the ale on the barrel by the porch near the Eidskallen Square to Fishery Island zipline.
1. Blow open the bridge truck crate and collect the Chili Chunks.
1. Kill a Necropincer with the fire trap to claim the claw, then on round 40 or later use the fishing rod to pull the red fish.
1. After placing the ingredients, use Jotunn Star kills around the dancing Mr. Peeks until the portal opens.

Field notes:
- Get the Chili Chunks from the bridge crate early, before the truck can create progression conflicts.
- Melee Macchiato speed and backpedal augments help the trial flow because you need to score zombie kills without being pinned.
- Golden Armor and a Pack-a-Punch IV Jotunn Star make the melee-only trial much safer.
- Treat the HVT zombie waves as spacing checks: chip the target, back out, then return for the melee kill instead of standing still.

## Wicked Relics

### Bus

- Map: Ashes of the Damned
- Difficulty: Medium
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 2 or 3 required.
- Effect: Zombie health regenerates.
- Portal: Blackwater Lake, north side of the boathouse.
- Save note: Safe before round 60.
- Trial: Only Brain-Rotted ally zombies can damage zombies. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 can bring Doppelghast and Zursa HVTs, with Mimics possible.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 60 before attempting the no-damage round.
1. Complete a full round without taking damage. You can kill zombies normally; the unlock checks whether you were hit, not whether you dealt damage.
1. Enter the Blackwater Lake portal after the no-damage round registers.

Field notes:
- Use whatever weapons, equipment, or traps help you finish the round safely; damage dealt by you does not invalidate the unlock.
- Wide training spaces and escape tools like Aether Shroud, Idle Eyes, Decoys, or Kazimirs help prevent a stray hit near the end of the round.
- Avoid self-damage, trap damage, fall damage, and explosive splash while attempting the no-damage round.
- Brain Rot augments that improve area damage, trigger rate, and ally damage speed up the trial dramatically.
- Use a high-rarity Pack-a-Punched assault rifle or SMG to trigger Brain Rot reliably, then stop shooting and let the ally zombie work.
- Do not panic-shoot HVT zombies if they are not Brain-Rotted; the trial rule only rewards the turned ally zombie damage.

### Dragon

- Map: Ashes of the Damned
- Difficulty: Hard
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 2 or 3 required.
- Effect: Ammo crates are disabled.
- Portal: Blackwater Lake, southeast wall of the south cabin.
- Save note: Safe before round 60.
- Trial: Explosive damage only. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 can bring Doppelghast and Zursa HVTs, with Mimics possible.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 60 or later, then complete the Cursed boss fight.
1. Enter the Blackwater Lake portal after the Cursed boss fight completion registers.

Field notes:
- Build perks early and try to make Quick Revive permanent before the Cursed boss fight.
- Bring Kazimirs or Monkey Bombs so Tessie repairs do not collapse during heavy pressure.
- Save Aether Shroud or another escape button for the final boss phase rather than spending it early.
- PhD Flopper protection is mandatory for comfortable close-range explosive play.
- Ray Gun, Kazimirs, and War Machines are the trial core. Craft extra War Machines before entering if your economy allows it.
- Use explosives to create space first, then focus HVT zombies once the normal wave is under control.

### Blood Vials

- Map: Ashes of the Damned
- Difficulty: Medium
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 2 or 3 required.
- Effect: All augments are disabled.
- Portal: Blackwater Lake, inside the cabin while facing west.
- Save note: Unsafe once the phone chain begins.
- Trial: Weapon damage is reduced by half. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 can bring Doppelghast and Zursa HVTs, with Mimics possible.

Unlock steps:
1. On rounds 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60, locate a ringing phone and answer it before ending the round.
1. Check the Janus Towers server room, Zarya control room, Blackwater Lake tool shed, Vandorn Farm first floor, Ashwood market, Exit 115 service areas, and Lost Cabins for the ringing phone.
1. Enter the Blackwater Lake cabin portal after the round-60 phone is answered.

Field notes:
- Hold several zombies on each phone round so you can search without accidentally moving to the next round.
- Listen for the ring while using jump pads to cover distance quickly.
- If the hold is taking too long, kill one zombie every few minutes so the round does not flip from despawn behavior while you search.
- Wonder Weapons and War Machines still handle the reduced-damage HVT zombies well because the damage penalty is more punishing for ordinary bullet weapons.
- Do not enter under-armored; the longer time-to-kill gives HVT zombies more chances to hit you.
- Because augments are disabled by the relic effect, plan the trial around raw weapon strength rather than build-specific perk bonuses.

### Golden Spork

- Map: Astra Malorum
- Difficulty: Medium
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 2 or 3 required.
- Effect: Incoming zombie damage is doubled.
- Portal: Crash Site, north of Quick Revive on the wall right of the Gobblegum machine.
- Save note: Safe after Mars is open and before round 60.
- Trial: Only hip-fire damage counts. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 can bring Klaus zombies, Mimics, Uber Klaus zombies, and an OSCAR HVT.

Unlock steps:
1. Open the Mars portal before round 60.
1. After round 60, equip a Mangler Cannon and bring Decoys to the Mars return portal.
1. Fire a Mangler Cannon shot into the portal that sends you back from Mars to the map.
1. Take the same portal yourself before the shot arrives.
1. On the map side, throw Decoys and pull as many zombies as possible near the portal exit.
1. After about 10 seconds, the Mangler Cannon shot follows you through the portal and detonates. Let that teleported shot kill the grouped zombies.
1. Enter the Crash Site portal after the teleported Mangler Cannon kill registers.

Field notes:
- Carry at least three Decoys so you can stack zombies where the portal shot exits.
- If the teleported Mangler Cannon shot does not kill enough zombies, rebuild the group and repeat the portal shot.
- Pack-a-Punch III LGM-1 and Ray Gun hip-fire well because they stay effective without aiming down sights.
- War Machines do not satisfy the hip-fire rule, so keep them for safety outside progress moments rather than expecting them to advance the bar.
- Maintain extra distance because the relic effect doubles incoming zombie damage, making small positioning mistakes much sharper.

### Civil Protector

- Map: Astra Malorum
- Difficulty: Medium
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 2 or 3 required.
- Effect: After enough eliminations, one perk is removed.
- Portal: Crash Site, upper level by Quick Revive on the left wall before the left door.
- Save note: Safe before round 60.
- Trial: Every zombie sprints. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 can bring Klaus zombies, Mimics, Uber Klaus zombies, and an OSCAR HVT.

Before you start:
- Equip Energy Mine before starting the Tessie light-code sequence.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 60, then activate Energy Mine next to Tessie.
1. Let Energy Mine get a kill near Tessie so the car turns on.
1. Watch Tessie flash her headlights three times. That signal means the light-code sequence is ready.
1. Read the four-light code Tessie flashes: Tessie's front-left light is A, front-right is B, back-left is C, and back-right is D.
1. Translate the letters to chandeliers in the Speed Cola room: A is front-left, B is front-right, C is back-left, and D is back-right.
1. Use PhD Flopper under the matching chandeliers in code order.
1. Enter the Crash Site portal after the chandelier sequence registers.

Field notes:
- Sliding can work for the chandelier sequence, but a direct PhD flop is usually easier to line up.
- Hold four or five zombies at the end of the round so the Tessie read is not rushed.
- Write down the four-letter code before moving to the chandeliers.
- Idle Eyes or Time Out can freeze the room long enough to perform the chandelier sequence without losing the code.
- Stamin-Up speed augments are worth more than usual because the entire arena is sprint pressure.
- The Apogee Annihilator helps erase normal zombies and lower HVT zombie health without needing long stationary aim windows.
- Keep moving through wide paths instead of defending a corner; the trial punishes every pause.

### Mangler Helmet

- Map: Paradox Junction
- Difficulty: Very Hard
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 2 or 3 required.
- Effect: The Arsenal is disabled.
- Portal: Green House balcony.
- Save note: Safe before round 60, but the Mr. Peeks Mystery Box sequence must be repeated after a reload.
- Trial: Survive six waves with reduced health and no armor. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 can bring a Doppelghast HVT or one or more Rad Hound HVT zombies.

Before you start:
- Do not activate the Seed relic for this run. Seed disables the Mystery Box, and this unlock requires the Mystery Box in both Pre-Nuke and Post-Nuke Nuketown.
- Equip the Mr. Peeks Field Upgrade before starting the Mystery Box sequence.

Unlock steps:
1. Charge the Mr. Peeks Field Upgrade.
1. In Pre-Nuke Nuketown, spin the Mystery Box until Mr. Peeks appears and the box is about to move.
1. Activate the charged Mr. Peeks Field Upgrade beside that Mystery Box so Mr. Peeks steals himself from the box and drops himself on the floor.
1. Pick up the dropped Mr. Peeks from the floor.
1. Repeat the same Mystery Box theft in Post-Nuke Nuketown. The Pre-Nuke and Post-Nuke pickups can be done in either order.
1. Place the two Mr. Peeks pickups on the mailboxes in front of the Yellow House in both Pre-Nuke and Post-Nuke Nuketown.
1. Reach round 60 and open the mailbox to take the ultra-rarity knife.
1. When the HVT Mimic spawns, kill it using only that knife.
1. Enter the Green House balcony portal after the HVT Mimic knife kill registers.

Field notes:
- Pack-a-Punch the ultra-rarity knife if needed, but do not mix in bullet or equipment damage on the HVT Mimic.
- Ray Gun or Sundergat should stay in hand whenever possible so you can clear space without armor.
- Make Quick Revive with Dying Wish permanent before attempting the trial.
- Extra health, Free Throw, Mask of Distraction, PhD slide movement, Kazimirs, and War Machines are all high-value safety tools.
- Keep distance at all times. Reduced health plus no armor makes greedy HVT zombie damage the fastest way to lose the run.

### Agarthan Device

- Map: Totenreich
- Difficulty: Medium-Hard
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 2 or 3 required.
- Effect: The map rotates a different zombie variant into each round.
- Portal: Dry Dock, upper west wall opposite the zipline.
- Save note: Unsafe after the Richtofen side-quest setup is completed.
- Trial: Zombies only count when killed indoors. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; later HVT zombie waves can include Necropincers, Doppelghasts, and Zursa.

Unlock steps:
1. Start the match as Richtofen.
1. Use the ARC-XD path into the Group 935 Genetic Lab, solve the labeled-jar puzzle, craft the Necrospike, and pick up the handheld radio from the cell area.
1. Use Tyr's Head exits until you collect Mr. Peeks from Ava's room and the helmet from the Requiem corpse room.
1. On round 15 or later, buy Melee Macchiato and open the Core Foundry barrel to start the Richtofen side sequence.
1. Wait for Von List to leave the chair, then place the collected items on it to complete the side setup.

Field notes:
- This unlock is character-locked, so confirm the operator before investing in the setup.
- The portal can currently be opened early, but the trial still behaves like a round-60-health arena, so do not enter underbuilt.
- Either hold upstairs Lighthouse with Toxic Growth or train patiently and pull HVT zombies indoors before landing the kill.
- Free Throw, Mask of Distraction, PhD slide movement, and War Machines are strong safety tools.
- If you open the portal early, spend the extra rounds building Ray Gun, Mustang and Sally, or Jotunn Star strength before starting the arena.

### Music Box

- Map: Totenreich
- Difficulty: Hard
- Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 2 or 3 required.
- Effect: Zombies only die to critical kills.
- Portal: Dry Dock, far west by the blocked staircase near Fjord Road.
- Save note: Safe before round 60.
- Trial: Activate six holdout circles marked by purple beams, then survive as each safe zone shrinks. The circle order is Fishery Island, Tyr's Foot, Dry Dock, Beacon Island, Eidskallen Square, then Skallen Market.

Unlock steps:
1. Reach round 60.
1. Enter Tyr's Head and land five zombie headshot kills in one visit.
1. Enter the Dry Dock portal after the headshot sequence registers.

Field notes:
- Save two zombies on a frost round if possible, then move to a spawn area close to your preferred Tyr's Head exit.
- For solo, stack zombies near Skallen Market with decoys, then use Kill Joy and Idle Eyes before entering Tyr's Head.
- A scoped Shadow SK plus Temporal Gift and Kill Joy creates the cleanest one-minute headshot window.
- If the trigger fails, rebuild the horde and repeat with a calmer entry rather than forcing shots through bad spacing.
- Cache Back, Shields Up, Kill Joy, and Kazimirs smooth the later holdouts.
- Save War Machine pressure for waves 5 and 6, where the safe zones are smallest and Necropincer pressure spikes.
- Do not be afraid to throw Kazimirs when boxed. The holdout fails from getting trapped more often than from slow progress.
- Frenzied Guard with extra armor and longer duration can stabilize the final circles if you prefer a defensive Field Upgrade.
