Games, characters, weapons, songs and timeline

  • World at War Zombies Maps, Easter Eggs - Call of Duty: World at War. Nacht der Untoten, Verrückt, Shi No Numa, and Der Riese.
  • Black Ops Zombies Maps, Easter Eggs - Black Ops expanded Zombies into a full mode with seven maps spanning the globe. The Aether storyline took shape as players traveled from Kino to the Pentagon, through the cosmodrome and Shangri-La, and ultimately to Moon where Richtofen enacted his grand scheme.
  • Black Ops II Zombies Maps, Easter Eggs - Black Ops II introduced the Victis crew navigating a post-apocalyptic Earth after the events of the Moon Easter Egg, and featured some of the most iconic maps of all time in Mob of the Dead, and Origins.
  • Black Ops III Zombies Maps, Easter Eggs - Black Ops III followed Primis across the multiverse to collect the souls of their Ultimis counterparts. Featured Gobblegums, Widow's Wine, four elemental bows in Der Eisendrache, and the conclusion of the cycle in Revelations.
  • Black Ops 4 Zombies Maps, Easter Eggs - Black Ops 4 ran two storylines in parallel: the Aether story concluded with Blood of the Dead and Tag der Toten, while the Chaos story introduced a new crew chasing artifacts through IX, Voyage of Despair, Dead of the Night, and Ancient Evil.
  • Cold War Zombies Maps, Easter Eggs - Cold War began anew with a new Dark Aether storyline. Requiem and Omega Group investigated dimensional breaches across four round-based maps and Outbreak. Introduced weapon rarity, armor, field upgrades, and an open progression system shared with multiplayer.
  • Black Ops 6 Zombies Maps, Easter Eggs - Black Ops 6 continued the Dark Aether storyline with Maya Aguinaldo and the Requiem team investigating the Order across six maps from Terminus to The Reckoning. It introduced Augments for perks, new Ammo Mods, and the return of round-based Zombies mechanics after MWZ.
