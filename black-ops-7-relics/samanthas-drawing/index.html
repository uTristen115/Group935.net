Samantha's Drawing Relic Tutorial - Black Ops 7 Zombies

Your weapon is exchanged each round for a comparable rarity and Pack-a-Punch level.

Map: Ashes of the Damned | Tier: Sinister | Difficulty: Easy

Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 1, 2, or 3 required.

Portal: Zarya Cosmodrome, along the path toward Yuri's Lab.

Save note: Unsafe once Chompy progress has started.

Unlock steps

  1. Before round 40, feed Chompy one weapon from each rarity tier: common, uncommon, rare, epic, and legendary.
  2. On round 40 or later, place a Wonder Weapon into the Chompy bin to finally open the portal.
  3. Enter the Zarya Cosmodrome portal after the Wonder Weapon deposit registers.

Trial rules and prep

Ammo Caches and Max Ammo drops are unavailable. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; waves 2 and 5 can lean heavily on Doppelghast HVTs, with Mimics possible.

  • Start with a grey pistol as early as possible so the common tier is not left until the round climb.
  • Listen for Chompy before depositing a weapon. If the bin is quiet, do not sacrifice an important gun into the wrong state.
  • Do the chain in one session. The rarity sacrifices are not reliable after saving.
  • Mule Kick with extra stock ammo makes the no-ammo-cache trial more forgiving.
  • Enter with full magazines and a weapon that has comfortable reserves, because a long HVT zombie wave can punish reload-heavy builds.
  • Keep Wonder Weapon ammo for HVT zombies instead of spending it on low-threat zombies.
