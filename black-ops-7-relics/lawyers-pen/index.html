Lawyer's Pen Relic Tutorial - Black Ops 7 Zombies

Loot-looking Mimic props can appear around the map. Approaching one can wake it up and turn a simple pickup check into a Mimic fight.

Map: Ashes of the Damned | Tier: Grim | Difficulty: Easy

Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode required; no Cursed Tier required.

Portal: Barnhouse south side, beside the left door.

Save note: Unsafe after candle progress begins.

Unlock steps

  1. Reach round 20.
  2. Light the Lost Cabins candle inside the ruined fog cabin.
  3. Light the Farm House candle at Vandorn Farm.
  4. Light the Ashwood candle on the shelf next to Juggernog.
  5. Enter the Barnhouse portal after all three candles are lit.

Trial rules and prep

Four Shock Mimic waves. Waves 1 and 3 are progress waves, while waves 2 and 4 ask you to burn down Mimic HVTs. Doppelghasts can join the HVT zombie waves, so do not tunnel only on the Mimic.

  • Molotovs and Napalm Burst are the safest candle tools because they light candles without forcing a special weapon setup.
  • The candles can be lit in any order. Keep one or two zombies alive so you can move cleanly between regions without the round flipping.
  • Avoid throwing equipment directly into Mimic shock bursts because the burst can erase equipment before it does useful work.
  • Brain Rot is the cleanest ammo mod here because Mimics are weak to it and turned zombies help control the cramped waves.
  • A medium-range Pack-a-Punched bullet weapon is enough; save explosives or scorestreaks for emergency HVT zombie pressure.
