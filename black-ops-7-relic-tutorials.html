Ashes of the Damned Relics

Ashes of the Damned has nine known Black Ops 7 relic tutorials: Lawyer's Pen, Dragon Wings, Teddy Bear, Vril Sphere, Samantha's Drawing, Focusing Stone, Bus, Dragon, and Blood Vials.

Lawyer's Pen Relic

GrimEasyAshes of the Damned
Effect
Fake drops can appear around the map and turn into Mimic encounters when approached.
Unlock
Reach round 20, then ignite three candles with Napalm Burst, a Molotov, or another fire source. Check the Vandorn Farm upstairs nightstand, the ruined fog cabin in Lost Cabins, and the shelf beside Juggernog in Ashwood.
Portal
Barnhouse south side, beside the left door.
Trial
Four shock-Mimic waves. Build progress on waves 1 and 3, then clear Mimic HVTs on waves 2 and 4.
Prep
Do not save after lighting the candles. Brain Rot is the cleanest ammo mod for the trial.

Dragon Wings Relic

GrimEasyAshes of the Damned
Effect
Standard power-up drops stop appearing, including special-round ammo drops.
Unlock
Reach round 20, ride the jump pad from Vandorn Farm toward Janus Towers, and shoot three purple targets in one airborne pass with a bullet weapon.
Portal
Northwest side of the Farmhouse.
Trial
Power-ups become hazards. Build progress on waves 1 and 3, then clear Mimic HVTs on waves 2 and 4.
Prep
Raise look sensitivity and bring a light, fast-handling bullet weapon.

Teddy Bear Relic

GrimEasyAshes of the Damned
Effect
The pause between rounds is heavily shortened.
Unlock
Reach round 20 and shoot ten Mr. Peeks bears around Ashes of the Damned, including Janus Tower Plaza, Vandorn Farm, Blackwater Lake, Ashwood Church, Rabbit Alley, Zarya Cosmodrome, Grounded Ship, Lost Cabins, and Exit 115.
Portal
Vandorn Farm, south wall of the Mystery Box building.
Trial
Every shot spends essence. Build progress on waves 1 and 3, then clear Mimic HVTs on waves 2 and 4.
Prep
Power Keg, a Necrofluid Gauntlet, and sawblade traps help keep the run efficient.

Vril Sphere Relic

SinisterEasyAshes of the Damned
Effect
The perk limit drops to four unless another reward bypasses the cap.
Unlock
Reach round 40, damage a Doppelghast without killing it, then lead it onto any jump pad so the launch and landing finish the enemy.
Portal
Zarya Cosmodrome, high middle road while looking east.
Trial
Purchases are disabled. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; waves 2 and 5 bring HVT pressure.
Prep
Buy essentials and craft equipment before entering.

Samantha's Drawing Relic

SinisterEasyAshes of the Damned
Effect
Your weapon is exchanged each round for a comparable rarity and Pack-a-Punch level.
Unlock
Before round 40, feed Chompy one weapon from each rarity tier from common through legendary. On round 40 or later, place a Wonder Weapon into the Chompy bin.
Portal
Zarya Cosmodrome, along the path toward Yuri's Lab.
Trial
Ammo Caches and Max Ammo drops are unavailable. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; waves 2 and 5 bring HVT pressure.
Prep
Feed a grey pistol early and wait for the Chompy audio cue before committing weapons.

Focusing Stone Relic

SinisterEasyAshes of the Damned
Effect
Self-revive kits are disabled.
Unlock
Reach round 40, kill a Zursa with a knife as the final hit, complete a legendary TEDD task for the second bottle, set both bottles at the Blackwater Lake wine bar, then shoot them in the shown burst order.
Portal
Zarya Cosmodrome, beneath the rocket while facing west.
Trial
Your essence is removed when the trial begins. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; waves 2 and 5 bring HVTs.
Prep
Watch the bottle sequence immediately after placement. A Pack-a-Punch III Necrofluid Gauntlet keeps the no-essence trial simple.

Bus Relic

WickedMediumAshes of the Damned
Effect
Enemy health regenerates.
Unlock
Reach round 60, then complete a full round without firing, meleeing, using equipment, or triggering PhD damage. Trap kills are the safest route.
Portal
Blackwater Lake, north side of the boathouse.
Trial
Only Brain-Rotted allies can damage enemies. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 can bring HVTs.
Prep
Use Vandorn Farm and Rabbit Alley sawblade traps, especially on a Ravager round.

Dragon Relic

WickedHardAshes of the Damned
Effect
Ammo crates are disabled.
Unlock
Reach round 60 or later, then complete the Cursed boss fight.
Portal
Blackwater Lake, southeast wall of the south cabin.
Trial
Explosive damage only. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 can bring HVTs.
Prep
Bring Kazimirs or Monkeys for Tessie repairs. PhD Flopper, Ray Gun, Kazimirs, and War Machines are strong trial tools.

Blood Vials Relic

WickedMediumAshes of the Damned
Effect
All augments are disabled.
Unlock
On rounds 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60, locate a ringing phone and answer it before ending the round.
Portal
Blackwater Lake, inside the cabin while facing west.
Trial
Weapon damage is reduced by half. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 can bring HVTs.
Prep
Hold several zombies on phone rounds and use jump-pad routes to listen across the map.

Astra Malorum Relics

Astra Malorum relic tutorials include Gong, Seed, Spider Fang, Matryoshka Doll, Golden Spork, and Civil Protector.

Gong Relic

GrimEasyAstra Malorum
Effect
Field Upgrades no longer recharge over time; Full Power becomes the main refill source.
Unlock
Reach round 20. Find rod-marked zombies and kill one near each charged bulb: Pack-a-Punch doorway, Luminarium doorway, and Machina Astralis or library doorway.
Portal
Outside Pack-a-Punch, left of the Gobblegum machine.
Trial
Only electric damage counts. Build progress on waves 1 and 3; expect Klaus pressure on wave 2 and an Uber Klaus threat on wave 4.
Prep
Dead Wire with Light Strike and Haste makes the bulb step more reliable.

Seed Relic

GrimEasyAstra Malorum
Effect
The Mystery Box is disabled.
Unlock
Reach round 20, pick up the grey pistol, and kill exactly the same number of enemies as the round number when the pistol was collected.
Portal
Observatory Dome, left side near the Ammo Cache.
Trial
Fight through four waves with a Pack-a-Punch I pistol. HVT waves can include Klaus units, a Mimic, and an Uber Klaus.
Prep
Avoid passive kill sources while counting. Use weapon stats to confirm the pistol kill count.

Spider Fang Relic

SinisterHardAstra Malorum
Effect
Perk prices stop falling over time.
Unlock
Keep the main quest untouched, use Wisp Tea so a wisp is active on OSCAR, then land the final hit on OSCAR with melee while the wisp condition is active.
Portal
Archives of Orbis, opposite Stamin-Up.
Trial
All perks are stripped when the trial starts. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; wave 2 can bring Uber Klaus, and wave 5 can bring OSCAR.
Prep
Use Wisp Tea duration control, a legendary Pack-a-Punch III knife, Idle Eyes, Insta-Kill, or Time Out for the final-hit window.

Matryoshka Doll Relic

SinisterEasyAstra Malorum
Effect
Salvage drops are cut down.
Unlock
Open the Mars portal before round 40. On Mars, place C4 on the three meat piles around the lower center stage, train a large group onto the stage, and detonate the C4 together.
Portal
Machina Astralis, by the Cryopod.
Trial
Kills only count while a Field Upgrade is active. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; later HVT waves can include Uber Klaus and OSCAR.
Prep
Aether Shroud and Frenzied Guard are the easiest Field Upgrade choices.

Golden Spork Relic

WickedMediumAstra Malorum
Effect
Incoming enemy damage is doubled.
Unlock
Open the Mars portal before round 60. After round 60, gather a large Mars horde on the ritual-circle area, fire the Mangler Cannon at the portal, and keep the horde on the circle as the blast resolves.
Portal
Crash Site, north of Quick Revive on the wall right of the Gobblegum machine.
Trial
Only hip-fire damage counts. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 bring HVT pressure.
Prep
Carry decoys for the Mangler Cannon setup. Pack-a-Punch III LGM-1 and Ray Gun hip-fire well.

Civil Protector Relic

WickedMediumAstra Malorum
Effect
After enough eliminations, one perk is removed.
Unlock
Activate Tessie with Energy Mine, read the headlight flash code, then use PhD Flopper under the matching chandeliers.
Portal
Crash Site, upper level by Quick Revive on the left wall before the left door.
Trial
Every zombie sprints. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 can bring Klaus, Mimic, Uber Klaus, and OSCAR HVT pressure.
Prep
Hold a few zombies to read the Tessie code calmly. Stamin-Up speed augments and the Apogee Annihilator help keep distance.

Paradox Junction Relics

Paradox Junction has Rocket Relic, Summoning Key, and Mangler Helmet tutorials.

Rocket Relic

GrimMediumParadox Junction
Effect
Scorestreaks cannot be used.
Unlock
Before round 20, pet a turned Rad Hound in past Nuketown. On round 20, move to future Nuketown and pet a D.A.W.G. scorestreak.
Portal
Past Nuketown, upstairs in Green House while facing south.
Trial
Enemies only take meaningful damage from elemental weaknesses. Progress waves are 1 and 3; HVT waves mix Rad Hounds, Doppelghasts, and Mimics.
Prep
Carry separate ammo mods: Shadow Rift for toxic zombies, Light Mend for Doppelghasts, and Brain Rot for Mimics.

Summoning Key Relic

SinisterMediumParadox Junction
Effect
Enemies explode when killed, and the blast can hurt you.
Unlock
Throw a grenade into the broken chimney beside the Destroyed Nuketown clock, use a low-rarity Brain Rot pistol on a dog round to recover the ball, then down yourself in the Green House backyard grave area on round 40 or later and place the collar and ball at the grave.
Portal
Yellow House, southwest outside wall.
Trial
Each wave has a timer, and every enemy must die before time expires. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; waves 2 and 5 mix Rad Hounds with Doppelghast HVTs.
Prep
Equivalent Exchange or Aftertaste can protect the intentional down. Ray Gun, Kazimirs, Free Throw, and War Machine pressure are strong timed-clear tools.

Mangler Helmet Relic

WickedVery HardParadox Junction
Effect
The Arsenal is disabled.
Unlock
Do not run the Seed relic. Charge Mr. Peeks, spin the Mystery Box in both Past and Destroyed Nuketown until the bunny appears, place both bunnies on matching Yellow House mailboxes, then on round 60 or later recover the ultra knife and kill the HVT Mimic using only that knife.
Portal
Destroyed Nuketown, Green House balcony.
Trial
Survive six waves with reduced health and no armor. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; waves 3 and 6 bring Doppelghast and Rad Hound HVTs.
Prep
Ray Gun or Sundergat should stay in hand whenever possible. Make Quick Revive with Dying Wish permanent before attempting the trial.

Totenreich Relics

Totenreich relic searches should resolve to Power Switch, Wrestler's Belt, Stuffed Elephant, Dancing Arnie, Agarthan Device, and Music Box.

Power Switch Relic

GrimVery EasyTotenreich
Effect
Your lethal and tactical equipment loadout changes at the start of each round.
Unlock
Reach round 20, read the deer-head altars, then strike the matching bear pelts with a Combat Axe in the correct order.
Portal
Blodheim Hall, south-wall bear pelt.
Trial
Trap kills only. Use map machinery through the short three-wave trial.
Prep
The pelt order has only twenty-four possible combinations if the altar read is unclear.

Stuffed Elephant Relic

SinisterMediumTotenreich
Effect
Health regeneration takes longer to begin.
Unlock
Reach round 40 without buying perks. Perkaholic before the round counter appears may work, but do not rely on it for a clean run.
Portal
Fishery Island, southeast corner behind the eastern building.
Trial
Find cursed eggs, destroy them, and kill the HVT before the timer ends. The objective can rotate through Necropincer, Doppelghast, Amalgam, and Zursa targets.
Prep
Fishery Island and Lighthouse holds are clean no-perk grind spots. Toxic Growth and a high-tier Jotunn Star help the climb.

Dancing Arnie Relic

SinisterEasyTotenreich
Effect
Perk machines become cursed and dispense random perks.
Unlock
Bring recipe items to the Blodheim Hall firepit in order: pot, ale, Chilly Chunks, Necropincer claw, and a round-40 fish. After placing ingredients, earn 100 melee kills near the firepit.
Portal
Fishery Island, northeast corner opposite the Mystery Box spot.
Trial
Melee kills only. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress; HVT waves can bring Necropincers first, then Necropincer and Zursa pressure later.
Prep
Melee Macchiato speed and backpedal augments help the trial flow. Golden Armor and a Pack-a-Punch IV Jotunn Star make the melee-only trial safer.

Agarthan Device Relic

WickedMedium-HardTotenreich
Effect
The map rotates a different zombie variant into each round.
Unlock
Play as Richtofen, use the RC-XD to open the Group 935 Genetic Lab uranium room, collect the walkie-talkie, recover Mr. Peeks and the helmet through Tyr's travel system, then complete the Richtofen side sequence after round 15 with Melee Macchiato.
Portal
Dry Dock, upper west wall opposite the zipline.
Trial
Enemies only count when killed indoors. Waves 1, 2, 4, and 5 build progress; later HVT waves can include Necropincers, Doppelghasts, and Zursa.
Prep
Hold upstairs Lighthouse with Toxic Growth or train patiently and pull HVTs indoors for the kill.

Music Box Relic

WickedHardTotenreich
Effect
Enemies only die to critical kills.
Unlock
Reach round 60, enter Tyr's Head, and land five headshots within one minute. For solo, stack zombies near Skallen Market with decoys, then use Kill Joy and Idle Eyes before entering the head.
Portal
Dry Dock, far west by the blocked staircase near Fjord Road.
Trial
Find the holdout circles and survive as the safe zone shrinks each wave. Early waves build progress; the final two waves are the cramped Necropincer-heavy finish.
Prep
Cache Back, Shields Up, Kill Joy, and Kazimirs smooth the later holdouts. Save War Machine pressure for waves 5 and 6.
