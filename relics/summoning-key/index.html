Summoning Key Relic Tutorial - Black Ops 7 Zombies

Zombies explode when killed, and the blast can damage you if you stay too close to the body.

Map: Paradox Junction | Tier: Sinister | Difficulty: Medium

Cursed requirement: Cursed Mode Tier 1, 2, or 3 required.

Portal: Yellow House, southwest outside wall.

Save note: Unsafe after collecting the dog collar.

Unlock steps

  1. In Pre-Nuke Nuketown, throw a grenade into the broken chimney near the clock and pick up the dog collar that drops.
  2. In the Yellow House backyard on Pre-Nuke Nuketown, use Brain Rot to turn a Rad Hound zombie.
  3. Let the turned Rad Hound zombie dig up the ball, then collect the ball.
  4. On round 40 or later, down yourself in the garden behind the Green House so the grave rises.
  5. Interact with the grave to place the dog collar and ball.
  6. Enter the Yellow House portal after the grave interaction opens it.

Trial rules and prep

Each wave has a timer, and every zombie must die before time expires. Waves 1, 3, and 4 build progress, while waves 2 and 5 can mix Rad Hound zombies with Doppelghast HVTs.

  • Equivalent Exchange or Aftertaste can protect the intentional down and keep the setup from costing the run.
  • A low-rarity Brain Rot pistol is less likely to kill the Rad Hound zombie before it digs up the ball.
  • Ray Gun, Kazimirs, Free Throw, and War Machine pressure are excellent for timed clears.
  • Keep distance after zombie kills during both setup and trial, because the relic effect turns close-range cleanup into chip damage.
