Black Ops 7 Zombies Relics Guide

All Black Ops 7 relics in the Group 935 archive, organized for players searching BO7 Zombies relic effects, unlock steps, portal locations, trial rules, save safety, map, tier, and prep notes.

The archive covers relics from Ashes of the Damned, Astra Malorum, Paradox Junction, and Totenreich.

All Black Ops 7 relics

  • Dragon Wings Relic - Ashes of the Damned. Standard power-up drops stop appearing, including special-round ammo drops, so ammo planning matters more on longer runs.
  • Lawyer's Pen Relic - Ashes of the Damned. Loot-looking Mimic props can appear around the map. Approaching one can wake it up and turn a simple pickup check into a Mimic fight.
  • Teddy Bear Relic - Ashes of the Damned. The pause between rounds is heavily shortened, giving less time to reload, plate, buy ammo, or rotate before the next spawn set begins.
  • Focusing Stone Relic - Ashes of the Damned. Self-revive kits are disabled.
  • Samantha's Drawing Relic - Ashes of the Damned. Your weapon is exchanged each round for a comparable rarity and Pack-a-Punch level.
  • Vril Sphere Relic - Ashes of the Damned. The perk limit drops to four unless another reward bypasses the cap.
  • Blood Vials Relic - Ashes of the Damned. All augments are disabled.
  • Bus Relic - Ashes of the Damned. Zombie health regenerates.
  • Dragon Relic - Ashes of the Damned. Ammo crates are disabled.
  • Gong Relic - Astra Malorum. Field Upgrades stop charging passively. You begin with a charge, but Full Power and other forced recharge tools become the main way back.
  • Seed Relic - Astra Malorum. The Mystery Box is disabled.
  • Matryoshka Doll Relic - Astra Malorum. Salvage drops are cut down.
  • Spider Fang Relic - Astra Malorum. Perk prices stop falling over time.
  • Civil Protector Relic - Astra Malorum. After enough eliminations, one perk is removed.
  • Golden Spork Relic - Astra Malorum. Incoming zombie damage is doubled.
  • Rocket Relic - Paradox Junction. Scorestreaks cannot be used.
  • Summoning Key Relic - Paradox Junction. Zombies explode when killed, and the blast can damage you if you stay too close to the body.
  • Mangler Helmet Relic - Paradox Junction. The Arsenal is disabled.
  • Power Switch Relic - Totenreich. Your lethal and tactical equipment changes at the start of each round, so long-term equipment planning becomes unreliable.
  • Wrestler's Belt Relic - Totenreich. Wall-buy weapons randomize each round.
  • Dancing Arnie Relic - Totenreich. Perk machines become cursed and dispense random perks.
  • Stuffed Elephant Relic - Totenreich. Health regeneration takes longer to begin.
  • Agarthan Device Relic - Totenreich. The map rotates a different zombie variant into each round.
  • Music Box Relic - Totenreich. Zombies only die to critical kills.
