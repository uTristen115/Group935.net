Zombies Perks and Machines

Browse Treyarch Zombies perks, machines, effects, Black Ops 7 variants, images, and archive notes.

  • Juggernog - Raises player health and gives mistakes a little more room to breathe.
  • Quick Revive - Speeds up revives, with solo self-revive behavior in several rule sets.
  • Speed Cola - Cuts reload time and keeps pressure weapons from going quiet too long.
  • Double Tap Root Beer - Improves rate of fire in early forms and adds extra projectile output in Double Tap II.
  • Stamin-Up - Raises movement speed and extends sprint stamina.
  • PhD Flopper - Protects against explosive and fall damage, with dive-triggered blast behavior in classic rules.
  • Mule Kick - Adds a third weapon slot.
  • Deadshot Daiquiri - Improves precision and headshot handling, with rule-set-specific aim bonuses.
  • Tombstone Soda - Creates a recovery path for gear or a second chance after a down, depending on the era.
  • Who's Who - Spawns a temporary duplicate that can revive the downed player.
  • Vulture Aid - Highlights useful objects and causes zombies to drop points, ammo, or concealment clouds.
  • Electric Cherry - Emits an electric burst while reloading, stronger when the magazine is low.
  • Widow's Wine - Converts grenades into web-like defensive bursts that slow attackers.
  • Der Wunderfizz - Dispenses a random or menu-selected perk, depending on the game rules.
  • Deadshot Dealer - BO4-era precision perk built around critical hits and accuracy.
  • Death Perception - Improves enemy awareness and highlights threats or resources by era.
  • Dying Wish - Prevents a lethal hit and grants a brief survival window before entering cooldown.
  • Victorious Tortoise - Improves shield protection and triggers a defensive blast when the shield breaks.
  • Stone Cold Stronghold - Rewards staying in a marked area with defensive and damage bonuses.
  • Winter's Wail - Releases frost bursts that slow or freeze attackers.
  • Timeslip - Speeds up equipment cooldowns, mystery box use, and several map interactions.
  • Ethereal Razor - Improves melee damage and range, especially in modifier-style perk setups.
  • Electric Burst - Creates electric effects tied to reload timing and weapon handling.
  • Zombshell - Creates debuff zones that slow or weaken nearby enemies.
  • Secret Sauce - Rolls a random perk into the selected slot.
  • Bandolier Bandit - Increases carried ammunition reserves.
  • Blaze Phase - Builds a charge while crouched, then releases a damaging dash.
  • Blood Wolf Bite - Summons Luna to attack nearby enemies after enough kills.
  • Elemental Pop - Adds random ammo mod effects to weapon fire.
  • PhD Slider - Turns a charged slide into an explosive impact and protects against self-damage.
  • Melee Macchiato - Improves melee power and utility in the Dark Aether ruleset.
  • Wisp Tea - Dark Aether perk brewed around spectral utility and escape pressure.
