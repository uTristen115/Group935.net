Ashes of the Damned Black Ops 7 Easter Egg Guide

Ashes of the Damned Black Ops 7 Zombies map guide with Easter Egg notes, relics, songs, images, and archive context.

Game: Black Ops 7 Zombies | Location: Dark Aether construct | Difficulty: 4/5

Ashes of the Damned Easter Egg tutorial

  • Ashes of the Damned Main Quest - Repair Ol' Tessie, install Pack-a-Punch, build the Necrofluid Gauntlet, brew the serum, unlock the Ashwood prism, clear the four area puzzles, collect the final wisps, and defeat Veytharion.

Ashes of the Damned relic guides

  • Dragon Wings Relic - Standard power-up drops stop appearing, including special-round ammo drops, so ammo planning matters more on longer runs.
  • Lawyer's Pen Relic - Loot-looking Mimic props can appear around the map. Approaching one can wake it up and turn a simple pickup check into a Mimic fight.
  • Teddy Bear Relic - The pause between rounds is heavily shortened, giving less time to reload, plate, buy ammo, or rotate before the next spawn set begins.
  • Focusing Stone Relic - Self-revive kits are disabled.
  • Samantha's Drawing Relic - Your weapon is exchanged each round for a comparable rarity and Pack-a-Punch level.
  • Vril Sphere Relic - The perk limit drops to four unless another reward bypasses the cap.
  • Blood Vials Relic - All augments are disabled.
  • Bus Relic - Zombie health regenerates.
  • Dragon Relic - Ammo crates are disabled.
